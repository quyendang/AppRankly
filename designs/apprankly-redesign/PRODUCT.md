# AppRankly personal prototypes

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
One app developer managing fewer than 20 Google Play and App Store listings.

## Product Purpose
See portfolio performance first, compare apps, then investigate changes and ASO opportunities.

## Operating Context
Personal Railway deployment; one owner; credentials belong only in server environment variables. This deliverable is an isolated prototype with synthetic data and no backend requests.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Five surfaces: portfolio, app detail, ASO, notebook/releases, settings. Keep analytics, ASO, deliberate AI actions, notes, releases and export. No teams, Git sync, public demo deployment or credential entry. Private analytics and notes require an explicit payload preview before an AI action.

## Brand Commitments
Keep AppRankly. User approved three separate directions: Pulse (sidebar, blue/coral), Spectrum (selected layout: horizontal navigation and comparison rows; user requested warm tones instead of violet/turquoise), Focus (app rail and master-detail, teal/orange). Fresh UI, no incumbent visual components. No gradients, glow or decorative glass. Vietnamese default, complete English alternative, light/dark/system.

## Evidence on Hand
Repository functions were inspected; no actual portfolio data or artwork was supplied. All names, counts and results in the prototypes are clearly labeled synthetic. Do not imply revenue coverage on Google or genuine retention/active-device measurement on Apple.

## Product Principles
- Make differences between apps easy to compare.
- Distinguish unavailable data from zero.
- Expose data freshness and failure recovery.
- Keep secrets outside the interface.
- Keep motion purposeful and implementation small.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Desktop 1440/1280, tablet 768 and mobile 390. Keyboard focus, reduced motion, Vietnamese glyphs, AA contrast. No paid AI or real credentials in prototypes.
